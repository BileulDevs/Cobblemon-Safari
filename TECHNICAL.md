# Cobblemon Safari: technical notes

How the mod works under the hood, for contributors, datapack makers and server owners who want the details. The player-facing presentation is in [README.md](README.md); every config key is explained in [CONFIG.md](CONFIG.md).

Every item of the mod (ticket, Escape Rope, gate, wall screen, decor) is in its own creative tab, "Cobblemon Safari" (`item/SafariCreativeTab`, registered by each loader: `FabricItemGroup` on Fabric, the creative tab registry event on NeoForge).

## Layout

| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| `common` | All gameplay logic, payloads, HUD, worldgen data |
| `fabric` / `neoforge` | Thin bridges from loader events to `SafariServerEvents` |
| `tools/worldgen` | Generator of every worldgen JSON file (do not hand-edit its output) |
| `example` | One example of everything that can be customized: config files (with a reference of every key), a datapack (spawns, NPCs, dialogue, gates, ruins) and a resource pack (texts, textures, sounds); see `example/README.md` |

## Worldgen

The Safari is a single island (shape radius 768 blocks, land to about 660, centred on 0,0) in an endless turquoise sea, enclosed by its own square world border. A small summit sits at the centre, ringed by rocky hills and a volcano. Around it, 14 habitats are laid out as sectors from the rocky ring to the coast, each with a whole sector of its own: grassland (north, where players arrive), savanna, dunes, badlands, jungle, marsh, mushroom grove, flower meadow, woodland, deep forest, taiga, snowfield, glacier, rocky hills.

The terrain is kept fairly even from the centre to the coast, so large builds have room: continentalness and erosion follow smooth radial profiles (`CONTINENT_PROFILE`, `EROSION_PROFILE`, through `safari/island_radial`) with only a little noise on top. The summit's peaks are limited to the inner tenth of the radius; the rest of the island is plains and rolling hills (about 65 to 120 blocks high), dropping to the sea over the last eighth of the radius.

Underneath:
- **Caves** everywhere, as in the overworld, without dripstone spikes (the cavern biome drops large and pointed dripstone).
- **The giant cavern**, under the summit (`GIANT_CAVERN`): not a dug shape but natural caves, the large pillared "cheese" caves of the Caves & Cliffs update grown into a cave system about 200 blocks across. Inside its zone the noise router adds a second set of cheese caves (vanilla's formula, on vanilla's cave noise without its two widest octaves, `noise/cavern`) with a lower threshold (`boost`) and less layering (`CAVERN_LAYER_CUT`), fading back to plain rock towards the edge of the zone (`CAVERN_FADE`, `CAVERN_EDGE`), so its caves merge into big chambers with pillars, ledges and pockets that look different on every seed, and stay a dozen blocks under the ground like vanilla's. A winding passage (`GIANT_CAVERN_TUNNEL`) starts on its east side, in a small cave room (`ROOM_LENGTH`, `ROOM_SCALE`) so it always leads somewhere, crosses the cavern, then climbs west and opens on the western slopes of the summit. It lies in the **deep cavern** biome (`deep_cavern`, Deep Dark climate without sculk), which also fills the deepest caves around it.
- **The lush cavern**, in the south (`LUSH_CAVERN`, around z 270), made the same way, in the lush grotto biome, with its own winding passage opening to the east (`LUSH_CAVERN_TUNNEL`), so the lush caves can be found without digging.
- Both are written to `safari/carved_caves` (min'ed into the final density, interpolated like the terrain) and kept dry: `safari/carved_caves_dry` lowers the router's fluid floodedness in and around them, so aquifers never fill them.

The layout relies on one Java density function type, `cobblemon_safari:island_coordinate`, which exposes distance and direction from the island centre; everything else is data. Sector borders are wobbled by noise (`CLIMATE_NOISE`), so no two maps look alike and no border is a straight line, while the smallest sector of any map keeps about three quarters of its nominal size.

The sky palette (turquoise zenith, golden horizon, coral sunsets, low clouds) comes from biome colours plus `SafariSkyEffects` on the client.

Run `python3 tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py` after changing biomes, tags, island size or terrain tuning. It also writes `park/ParkGeometry.java` (park centre, border half-size, arrival point and spread, rest house anchors), so the border, the arrival and the rest houses always match the generated terrain: there is nothing to copy into a config file. It only rewrites the folders it owns; the Safari Gate's structure files are left alone.

## Biome tags (spawn contract)

Habitat tags: `safari_plains`, `safari_savanna`, `safari_desert`, `safari_badlands`, `safari_jungle`, `safari_swamp`, `safari_meadow`, `safari_forest`, `safari_deep_forest`, `safari_taiga`, `safari_snow`, `safari_ice`, `safari_rocky`, `safari_mushroom`, `safari_mountain`, `safari_volcanic`, `safari_lake`, `safari_river`, `safari_beach`, `safari_lush_cave`, `safari_deep_cave`.
Aggregates: `safari_cave`, `safari_grassland`, `safari_wooded`, `safari_water`, `safari_wetland`, `safari_arid`, `safari_frozen`, `safari_highland`, `is_safari`.

Use them in Cobblemon spawn files as `"biomes": ["#cobblemon_safari:safari_forest"]` to add Pokémon to the park only.

Each Safari biome is also bridged into the matching Cobblemon habitat tags (`data/cobblemon/tags/worldgen/biome`, generated from `COBBLEMON_TAG_BRIDGE`), so Cobblemon's natural spawns and spawn datapacks populate the park on their own. The snowfield also takes Cobblemon's snowy forest and snowy taiga tags, and the glacier its frozen ocean tag: with only `is_snowy` and `is_tundra`, the snowfield would have about six species one can meet without bait (Swinub, Stantler, Sentret and Cetoddle only come to a Poké Snack in Cobblemon). The volcano also takes Cobblemon's Nether sub-tags (`nether/is_basalt`, `is_wasteland`, `is_mountain`, `is_soul_fire`, `is_soul_sand`), so the ashen and fiery Nether fauna lives there (Litleo, Ponyta, Litwick, Duskull...); it does not take `#minecraft:is_nether`, which would also bring nether fortresses and nether ruined portals to the island, so species spawning only through that tag (one of Gastly's spawns, for instance) stay out of the volcano. The crimson, warped, fungus, forest, frozen and overgrowth Nether tags are left out too. The deep cavern takes `is_deep_dark`, Cobblemon's Deep Dark tag. The volcano also takes the badlands tag, the mushroom grove the magical one and the flower meadow the plains one, so every surface habitat has 30 to 130 species one can meet without bait. Catch-all tags such as `#cobblemon:is_overworld` are left out of surface habitats on purpose so habitats stay distinct; the underground cavern alone takes `is_overworld`, because Cobblemon spawns its cave Pokémon (Zubat, Geodude, Onix, Gible... about 126 species) through it with underground conditions, exactly as in any cave of a normal Cobblemon world. Caves are not surface habitats (`SafariBiomes.SURFACE_HABITATS`), so this changes nothing for the specials, the shop or the bounties. Two sections of the config filter what comes in, one per way in (`spawn/SpawnSource`): `spawns` for Cobblemon's natural spawns, `featured` for the specials (the board's draw and the mod's spawner). Both inherit the same rules (`SafariConfig.SpeciesRulesSection`): legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox Pokémon each have a section (`legendary`, `mythical`, `ultraBeast`, `paradox`) with `allowSpawn` (refused by default, except paradox Pokémon, allowed) and `maxSpawnPerPokemon`, the most Pokémon of each of their species coming in that way per rotation of the specials (1 by default, 0 for no cap, which is the paradox default), plus `excludedSpecies`. Each way counts apart, so caps of 1 in both allow one natural Rayquaza and one special Rayquaza per rotation. A slot is held by each one caught in the Safari during the rotation and by each one still roaming; one that leaves uncaught (despawned, fled, knocked out) gives its slot back within about ten seconds, so a missed Rayquaza can come back for another player (`spawn/SpecialSpawnCounter`, which sweeps the roaming ones every 5 s; saved in `data/cobblemon_safari_special_spawns.dat`, format 2, which also keeps each roaming Pokémon's way and Pokémon UUID so its capture is charged to the right count). A same-species cap keeps one Pokémon from flooding a habitat. A config at version 1, where `spawns` governed both ways, is converted on load by copying its rules to `featured`.

Refusing a natural spawn in Cobblemon's spawn event (`spawn/NativeSpawnRules`) comes too late: Cobblemon has already picked the species by weight, so a refused species with a huge weight (a datapack spawning Mew as common with weight 999999) would win nearly every attempt and leave the park empty. `spawn/SafariSpawnFilter`, a spawning influence put on each player's spawner while they are in the Safari, answers Cobblemon's `affectSpawnable` while it lists the candidates, and takes out the species refused by `spawns`, those at their natural cap and those already `maxSameSpeciesNearby` times around the player; they also vanish from `/checkspawn` there. The forbidden set is rebuilt when the config, the datapacks or the species list change; the capped and crowded sets are snapshots refreshed once a second (crowding is counted around the player rather than around each candidate spot, which would cost one entity query per spot). The spawn event filter stays behind it, for the second the snapshots lag, for a spot more crowded than the player's surroundings, and for spawners that do not carry the influence (Poké Snacks, other mods).

## Special Pokémon of the day

Every rotation (4 h by default, `featured.rotationMinutes`, aligned on midnight UTC: 0, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 h UTC), the mod draws a few uncommon, rare and ultra-rare species (by default 1, 2 and 3: `featured.uncommonSlots`, `rareSlots`, `ultraRareSlots`) from what is actually installed: Cobblemon's spawn data decides each species' habitat and rarity, and species without a natural spawn get a habitat from their types. The draw is deterministic per world and rotation, mixes rarities, and prefers different habitats. A new rotation, natural or forced with `/safari rotate`, never brings back a special of the rotation before (unless nothing else can fill a slot): the last specials drawn and the ones their draw left out are kept in the world state (`specials`, `specials_excluded`), so a restart or reload redraws the same list. Starter lines (any species whose first stage carries Cobblemon's `starter` label: Cyndaquil, Quilava and Typhlosion alike) and the Eevee line (Eevee and its eight evolutions) get a slot only one time in four between them (`FeaturedRotation.CROWDING_SLOT_CHANCE`, `SpeciesRules.isCrowdingLine`) when other species of the same rarity are available: Cobblemon files most starters as ultra-rare, where the 27 lines would otherwise take most of the slots, and the eeveelutions land together in one rarity too. The mod spawns these specials itself, near players and only in their habitat, with caps so none takes over. They keep the habits of their species: each natural spawn's hours, light, sky light and sky conditions in Cobblemon's spawn files are kept per habitat (`spawn/SpawnWindow`), and a special only appears where and when one of them is met, so a nocturnal species comes out at night only and one that wants darkness waits for it. Specials appear at the surface, so a spawn that needs to be out of the sky (a cave spawn) is read as "at night" instead (`SpawnWindow.of`); otherwise a cave dweller drawn as a special would never show up. Species without natural spawns (habitats from their types) appear at any time. Weather, moon phase and other conditions are not checked. The list is shown by the wall screens, by the operators' `/safari today` and by the NPCs; it is not announced in chat on entry.

### Safari Wall Screen

`cobblemon_safari:safari_wall_screen`: a live display on a panel held off the wall by one arm; up to 3 side by side merge, with the arm in the middle. Right-click with an empty hand opens its settings (who may: `block/WallScreenSettings`): what it shows (specials, the viewer's stats, missions) and its tilt, a slider from -30° (facing the ceiling) to +30° (facing the floor) by 5°. The tilt is the block state `tilt` (0 to 6 steps) plus `upward` for the ceiling side: block state integers cannot be negative.

The FEATURED page shows the current specials with their animated Cobblemon models, rarity and habitat (scrolling when too long), and a progress bar: by default a viewer with a session sees their own hunting time drain on the specials and missions screens and the time until the next rotation on the record screens, anyone else sees the next rotation everywhere; each player chooses per kind of screen in client.json (`board.featuredFooter`, `statsFooter`, `bountiesFooter`: `SESSION` or `ROTATION`, `config/WallScreenFooter`). Specials are split over pages when they do not fit. Everything is drawn client side from data the server pushes (`SafariFeaturedPayload`), so a screen works anywhere, in any dimension, without configuration; the picture is painted by `client/board/SafariScreenPainter`. Behind the panels, the screen shows its own pixel-art landscape (a watering hole at the foot of the volcano, `textures/gui/board_background_<light>.png`, drawn by `tools/gui_assets.py`) in the light of the time of day in game (morning, dusk or night), dimmed and cropped around the horizon; `board.backgroundImage` turns it off (plain `board.colors.background` colour) and `board.panelOpacity` (0 to 1, default 0.72) sets how much the title band, cells and bars hide it. `board.useSprites` chooses how the Pokémon are drawn: `false` (default, turning 3D Cobblemon models) or `true`, the mod's own pixel sprites, `assets/cobblemon_safari/textures/pokemon_sprites/<species>[_<form>][_shiny].png` (`client/board/PokemonSprites`), which a resource pack can override with the same names; a Pokémon without a sprite keeps its model. `python3 tools/pokemon_sprites_pack.py <folder> --mod` installs them from a folder laid out as `<dex>_<name>/<name>[_<form>][_shiny].png` (or `<folder> <pack.zip>` makes a resource pack instead). Every colour of the screen can be changed in `config/cobblemon_safari/client.json` (`board.colors` section, `#RRGGBB` or `#AARRGGBB`; a colour with its own alpha keeps it over the landscape). The wall screens' section of the client config is `board`. The rotation length is `featured.rotationMinutes` in `config/cobblemon_safari/server.json`.

## Decor

Furniture for rest houses and gates, in the mod's creative tab (`decor/SafariDecor`). Textures are drawn by `tools/decor_textures.py`, models, block states, loot tables and the axe tag by `tools/decor_assets.py`.

- **Safari Banner** (`safari_banner`, `safari_wall_banner` on a wall; one item places both, like vanilla banners): a pole, a bar and a cloth swaying like a vanilla banner's, same size, drawn by `client/banner/SafariBannerRenderer`. The cloth shows a picture chosen with right-click and an empty hand (`client/banner/BannerDesignScreen`, a grid of thumbnails with a scrollbar when they overflow, same rights as the wall screens, `decor/BannerDesigns`), saved in the block entity and sent to clients in its update packet. A picture is any texture under `textures/block/banner/`, in any namespace (32 × 64 for the shipped ones, any 1:2 size works): vanilla's block atlas takes everything under `block/`, so a resource pack adds pictures by dropping PNGs there, and a `.png.mcmeta` animates one (frames stacked vertically, `"width"` and `"height"` giving the frame size, as the animated ones shipped do: `river_waves`, `fireflies`, `waterfall`, `campfire`, `rainstorm`, `windy_grass`, `lily_pond`, `shooting_stars`, `sun_rays`, `spinning_ball` (a capture ball turning on itself), `ball_throw` (a ball thrown at an original creature, which it catches after three wobbles); a picture may pick its own frame count and frame time). Names come from `cobblemon_safari.banner_design.<namespace>.<file>`, else from the file name. Pictures are drawn by `tools/banner_textures.py`. A broken banner drops a plain banner: the picture is chosen again once placed.
- **Coat rack** (`coat_rack`): two blocks tall, a ranger's jacket on its front hook and a ranger's hat on top; a dye recolours the hat (block state `hat`, 16 colours, brown when placed; same rights as the wall screens). Built like vanilla's tall plants; only the lower half has loot, so breaking either half drops one rack.

## Rest houses

Pokémon Centers built once per map, always (they are the only place to heal inside the park), door toward the island centre. Each is the structure template `data/cobblemon_safari/structure/safari_pokecenter.nbt` (20 × 11 × 19, door on its west side as saved), placed by `structure/RestHouseBuilder`: turned so its door faces the centre, its ground layer level with the median ground under it (trees and plants looked through), on a foundation, with the volume above it and a one-block ring cleared first (trees, hill). The terrain around is reshaped over `SKIRT` (10) blocks: level with the house for `FLAT_RING` (2) blocks, then sloping back to the natural ground (smoothstep, rounded corners), cut down or filled up with each column's own top and second blocks; water is left alone and a column next to a pond is never cut below it. The template's grass and dirt layer takes the ground of the site (its most common top and second blocks: sand in the dunes, stone on the peaks, mycelium in the mushroom grove), the foundation too, and where most of the ground had snow, the open ground of the house and the reshaped ground get a snow layer; the planters keep their grass. Every tree reaching into the volume to clear is felled whole first (its logs found through its leaves, as vanilla measures leaf distance; a group running beyond the build area, or over 4,096 logs, is left), instead of being cut flat against the house; the trees the reshaping undermines and the leaves left without a log are removed afterwards, as around the gates (`gate/GateClearing`, with the house itself left out: its beams are logs). Blocks are set without reshaping their neighbours (`UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE`, and `setKnownShape` for the template): otherwise a reshaped flower, petal or tuft of grass that lost its support would break and drop as an item, Minecraft forcing the drop on neighbours whatever the flags. As a safety net, items younger than the build lying around the house are removed once it is done. Since no neighbour is updated, the vines of removed trees would stay hanging in the air: once the trees are gone, every vine of the area is given the shape vanilla would give it (`Block.updateFromNeighbourShapes`, top down so a vine hanging from a removed one goes too), and those left with nothing to hold are removed; this applies around the gates too. The fossil machine of the template is placed unlinked (its multiblock links were absolute positions of the world it was saved in), so it stands as decoration. Cobblemon blocks that ignore Minecraft's template rotation (TM machine, fossil machine, disc shelf) are turned by the builder itself, so they face the room whatever the house's orientation. The template uses only blocks of Minecraft 1.21.1 and Cobblemon 1.8.0: a block unknown to the running game is placed as air. There are eight, one per anchor of `park/ParkGeometry.REST_HOUSES`, generated with the terrain by `tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py` (`ENTRANCE_HOUSE_NORTH_OF_ARRIVAL`, `REST_HOUSE_RING`, `REST_HOUSE_BEARINGS`): the entrance just north of the arrival point, then seven on the arrival's ring, every 45 degrees from east (east, south and west first, then the four diagonals). They are not in the server config, so they always match the island. Each anchor is a starting point: `structure/RestHouseSites` reads the terrain the generator will produce (without generating chunks) on a grid around it and keeps the flattest dry spots inside the park (checked again for water on a 4-block grid of the whole footprint), widening the search to twice then four times its radius when nothing passes; then each spot, best first, is checked for water on the real terrain under the house and two blocks around it, and the first dry one is built on (the driest if all are wet, logged). Spots are at least 64 blocks from the other houses and clear of the ruin and tower spots; the entrance is searched within 32 blocks of its anchor first, the others within 64. Where each house went is saved in the world state. Inside, as built in the template: two Cobblemon Healing Machines behind the counter that never run out of charge (saved infinite), a Cobblemon PC, a cooking corner with a Poké Pot on a Cobblemon campfire, the wall screens, Safari Banners (the ranger compass and the paw trail on a wall, the Safari crest on each side of the door, their pictures saved in the template's block entities), a coat rack with a green hat in a corner, a TM machine and vanilla bookshelves. `SafariProtection` refuses every click on a Cobblemon disc shelf in the Safari, except for exempt creative players, for templates replaced by a datapack. When: at server start every house gets its site (searches in parallel on background threads, noise only) and only the entrance house is built, its chunks generated on the spot, since players arrive in front of it. The others are built in the background shortly after the start (see Safari Guide and trips below), or the first time a player has loaded every chunk around them (`RestHouses.tick`, a look every second), whichever comes first, one at a time, in steps over a few ticks (`RestHouseBuilder.Job`: survey, foundation and clearing, terrain in slices of 400 columns, template, then the cut trees). Building all eight at start would mean generating some 150 chunks on the server thread, about 25 seconds before a new map could be played. A planned house's spot is kept clear of spawns like a built one, and its music zone is known to clients; if the real terrain shows water there, the next site of its list is used (the last one is built on whatever it holds). Sites still to build are saved in the world state (`house_plans`). A layout change shows after the next map reset. Spawns keep clear of the whole footprint, steps included (`RestHouses.FOOTPRINT_REACH`). Players entering the Safari (and those sent back in by a map reset) arrive on the grass in front of the entrance house's steps, on the axis of its door, facing it (`RestHouses.entranceArrival`); only when that spot is not safe ground (the house not built yet, something placed there) do they arrive at a random dry spot around the arrival point.

In the Safari, right-clicking a Cobblemon station (PC, Healing Machine, campfire) always works whatever the player holds, and a crouching click on a campfire is refused so the Poké Pot stays.

### Safari Guide and trips

Each rest house also has a Safari Guide (NPC class `safari_guide`, dialogue `data/cobblemon_safari/dialogues/safari_guide.json`, skin `textures/npcs/safari_guide.png`), in the south hall in front of the slab counter (`RestHouseBuilder.GUIDE_X`/`GUIDE_Z`, facing the hall), kept like the clerk by `structure/RestHouseStaff`, and removed when `restHouseTravel.guideEnabled` is off. He takes a player to any other rest house of the island. His dialogue has one option per compass direction seen from the centre of the island (`structure/Compass`), shown when a house lies there and it is not the one the player stands in (`q.player.safari_guide_can_<direction>()`, `safari_guide_go_<direction>()`); each option is named by the house's biome, through a dialogue text type of the mod (`{"type": "cobblemon_safari:rest_house_name", "direction": "north"}`, `teleport/RestHouseNameText`), with the direction added when two houses share a biome ("Jungle Safari (north)"). The biome of a house not built yet is read from the generator's noise, so naming loads no chunk.

A trip (`teleport/RestHouseTravel`, also used by the Escape Rope's rest house option) is not instant. The destination's area gets a chunk ticket (`RestHouses.AREA_TICKET_RADIUS`, 3 chunks around the house): new chunks are generated off the server thread, and a planned house builds itself as soon as its area is loaded, as it would for a player standing there. Meanwhile the player watches a cinematic (`client/travel/TravelCinematicScreen`, driven by `network/SafariTravelPayload`, wire version 2: START, SHOT, END), between two black bands with "On the way to" and the destination. It plays one of three scenes. The guide's trips are filmed by default (`restHouseTravel.cinematicStyle`: `FLIGHT`, see below); with `PANORAMA`, or when the flight cannot be staged, they show the expedition trail of the missions screens, in the light of the time of day, slowly panning. The Escape Rope's trips are filmed too; with `PANORAMA`, or when its flight cannot be staged, they play a pixel-art scene (`client/travel/CaveEscapeAnimation`, atlas drawn by `tools/travel_cave_escape.py`): a trainer walks out of a cave, jumps on a bird's back and flies off, then flies on above the clouds until the trip ends; the trainer and the bird are original designs. It cannot be closed with Escape, and it closes by itself if the server never answers. The player is moved once the house stands, its arrival is loaded and the cinematic has run for `restHouseTravel.cinematicSeconds` (3 s); the screen then fades out on the new place. A trip follows its house, ticket included, when a build finds the spot wet and moves to the next candidate; it is given up after 45 s, during a battle, or when the player leaves the Safari. Trips start the per-player wait `restHouseTravel.cooldownSeconds` (30 s, the guide and the rope share it, kept in memory), are refused during a battle, and the guide's only work within 6 blocks of a guide, so another dialogue cannot use the functions as a free teleport.

**Flight cinematic.** A client moves the vehicle it controls by itself, so the server cannot script the player's own mount; instead a double flies (`teleport/TravelFlight`). On the front steps of the house the player leaves (for the rope, where it was used: in the open air, the player's own spot; under cover, swimming or in the air, the best open-air spot found by `SafariTeleporter.findStandingSpotNear`, 12 blocks around at most, to which the player is brought under a black screen held for 1 s so its surroundings reach the client; no such spot, or no open sky there, keeps the pixel-art scene), a mount (no AI, no gravity, uncatchable, unbattleable, invulnerable) takes off carrying an NPC of class `cobblemon_safari:safari_traveller`, named like the player and wearing their skin, seated with Cobblemon's own riding pose (poser `safari_traveller`). The mount is a copy (species, form, aspects, gender, level; the party Pokémon itself is never sent out) of the first non-fainted Pokémon of the player's party that Cobblemon lets ride in the air (an AIR riding behaviour and at least one seat), or a Staraptor when the party has none. The server moves the mount along a fixed path each tick (a 2-block rise, then a climb 40 blocks away and 26 up, 4 s), toward the destination or, when roofs or trees block that path (heightmap checked at 13 points), the nearest open compass direction; no open direction gives the panorama instead. The client's camera stands still beside the steps (the best of 24 candidate spots: free air is required and the hall of a rest house refused; among the rest, a spot under the open sky that sees both the steps and the middle of the path is preferred, then one that sees the steps, then any open-sky spot, then one under a canopy) and turns to follow the mount (`client/travel/TravelCamera`, applied by `mixin/client/CameraTravelMixin` at the end of `Camera.setup`), with the HUD and the hand hidden (the player's F1 setting is restored afterwards). The screen goes black as the mount leaves, the trip waits for the destination as usual, then the player is moved, invisible, to its arrival; 1.5 s later, under the black, a second mount lands there with the double, filmed the same way, and the actors are removed when the player takes their place. The skin comes from the player's signed login profile, downloaded once per server run on the game's I/O pool (`teleport/TravelSkins`; Cobblemon's `loadTexture` would download on the server thread); offline-mode servers have none and the double wears the standard trainer look. The player is invulnerable during any trip; while flying they are invisible, but their armour and held items still show to others, as for any invisible player. Actors are tagged `cobblemon_safari.travel_actor`; tagged entities that belong to no flight under way (left by a crash) are removed every 5 s.

So that trips rarely wait, `structure/RestHousePrebuilder` builds the houses of a new map in the background (`restHouseTravel.prebuildHouses`): from 30 s after the server starts, it puts the same kind of ticket on the next unbuilt house, waits for the build to end (3 minutes at most, then that house is skipped until the next restart), and moves on.

## Ruins and towers

Every map has Cobblemon ruins and Gimmighoul towers, through the Safari's own structures (`worldgen/structure/safari_ruins/`, written by `tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py`, `SAFARI_STRUCTURES`) that reuse Cobblemon's template pools: towers in six looks (temperate, lush, rooted, deserted, sunscorched, frozen) and ruins standing above ground (henges, toppled pillars, shaded ruins, a desert town centre, a frozen altar, an overgrown trial), each limited to the habitats it suits. Two structure sets spread them: a tower candidate every 320 blocks and a ruin candidate every 224 blocks, so a map has several of each in practice. They are generated with the terrain, so they blend in like in the overworld, and change with the daily map.

## Ambience

The Safari has its own music: six original tracks composed and rendered by `tools/music/compose_safari_music.py` (pure synthesis, no samples, deterministic) into `assets/cobblemon_safari/sounds/music/`. Each biome plays the track of its zone (`HABITAT_SOUNDS` in `tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py`): `plains` (grassland, savanna, flower meadow, water), `forest` (woodland, deep forest, taiga, jungle, marsh, mushroom grove), `arid` (dunes, badlands, rocky hills, volcanic), `snow` (snowfield, glacier, peaks) and `caves`. Vanilla music is never heard in the Safari. Tracks are 1 to 3 minutes apart, and crossing into another zone lets the current track finish.

Inside a rest house the `center` theme starts at once (`client/music/SafariMusic`, through `MinecraftMusicMixin` on `Minecraft.getSituationalMusic`); it loops while the player stays inside. The client knows where the houses are from the rules payload. Creative players hear the zone music too, instead of vanilla's creative music.

The ambient sound of each biome is the cry of a Pokémon one can actually meet there (common spawns of the habitat's bridged tags in Cobblemon 1.8.0, no bait needed), a few species per biome, played quietly at random (`additions_sound`, chance 0.004 per tick, about once every 12 seconds). These `ambient.<biome>` events point at Cobblemon's own cry files.

To change the music: edit `THEMES` in the composer, run it, then run `generate_worldgen.py` (which checks every zone has its track and rewrites `sounds.json` and the biomes).

## Protection

Inside the Safari, by default: players take no damage (except /kill and the void), cannot break or place blocks or use block-changing tools, fire never spreads, and explosions destroy no block. Creative and spectator players are exempt from the terrain rules. Blocks listed in `protection.placeableBlocks` (by default Cobblemon's Poké Cake and Poké Snack) can still be placed and picked up. Breaking a protected block is refused only when it would break, so mining shows its cracks and the warning appears once. Each rule can be switched off in the `protection` section of the config.

## Safari Game loop

- **Entry**: always paid with banked Safari time, which only tickets (or `/safari credit add`) provide: walk into a Safari Gate, talk to its warden, `/safari enter` (op), or any custom access method through `SafariAccess`. A ticket can carry its own duration: `/give @p cobblemon_safari:safari_ticket[custom_data={duration_seconds:600}]`. Used inside, a ticket extends the running session.
- **Poké Balls**: players bring their own (the balls listed in `allowedBalls`, Safari Balls by default, which Cobblemon crafts from apricorns). The shop gives 30 free Safari Balls once a day, theirs to keep (see the shop below). A player entering without any allowed ball is told so on entry, with the rest houses' clerk to turn to. The games' loan is still there for servers that want it, off by default (`loan.count`, 0): lent balls are handed out on entry, taken back on exit, only work inside the Safari and are reclaimed from any player found outside with one.
- **HUD**: the time left only, under a Safari Ball turned stopwatch (white dial, gold crown and pusher; `textures/gui/safari_timer.png`, drawn by `tools/timer_icon.py`, replaceable by a resource pack) whose red hand turns around the dial, faster and faster as time runs out (one turn in 8 s with 15 minutes or more left, about one a second at 5 minutes, two a second at the end; eight crisp positions drawn by `SafariTimerHud`, the stopwatch itself stays still), with the time under it, in the bottom left corner by default, the two lines together as high as the hotbar and level with it (`hudAnchor` BOTTOM_LEFT, `hudOffsetX/Y` 20 and -16 place the middle of the widget, 5 px above the bottom of the screen, in `config/cobblemon_safari/client.json`). In chat, the time left is given on entry, at each of `warningThresholdsSeconds` (15 min, 10 min, 5 min, 1 min, 30 s and 10 s by default; white, yellow from 5 minutes, red from 1 minute, by the time left), when a ticket extends the hunt, and on `/safari`.
- **Recap** at the end of each game, and lifetime records: `/safari stats [player]`, `/safari top`.
- **Safari Mark**: every Pokémon caught in the Safari gets the mark `cobblemon_safari:mark_safari` ("the Safari Find", `capture/SafariMark`, `capture.awardSafariMark`). It is plain Cobblemon mark data (`data/cobblemon_safari/marks/mark_safari.json`, icon `textures/gui/mark/mark_safari.png` drawn by `tools/safari_mark_icon.py`), so Cobblemon loads, saves and syncs it; its `chance` is 0, so Cobblemon never rolls it on a wild spawn. It is added at the end of the capture (lowest priority) and made the active mark only when the Pokémon has none, so a mark Cobblemon gave on the same capture keeps its place. A datapack can replace the file to change its colour or texture.
- **Game statistics**: the Safari also has its lines in the game's Statistics screen (General tab), as custom statistics `cobblemon_safari:<name>` (`stats/SafariStatistics`, registered on both sides with their formatter): `hunts`, `time_hunted` (shown as time), `pokemon_caught`, `shiny_caught`, `specials_caught`, `best_hunt` (a record, raised at the end of each hunt), `points_earned` (captures and missions only, not shop refunds or `/safari points add`), `points_spent` (shop and mission swaps), `missions_completed` and `rest_house_trips` (Guide and Escape Rope). Scoreboards can read them as `minecraft.custom:cobblemon_safari.<name>`. They are saved by the game with each player's statistics (`stats/<uuid>.json`); on login, those the lifetime record also holds (hunts, time hunted, catches, shinies, best hunt) are raised to it, so a player of an older version starts with their history. Hunts and time hunted move when a hunt ends, the others as they happen.

## Configuration files

Everything lives in `config/cobblemon_safari/`: `server.json` (rules of the Safari, server side), `shop.json` (the shop) and `client.json` (each player's display preferences). `/safari reload` re-reads `server.json` and `shop.json`. A file that cannot be parsed (a typo, a missing brace) is never overwritten: the mod runs on the defaults and says so in the log until the file is fixed, even if those defaults would otherwise be saved after a correction (`JsonConfigFile.unreadable`).

Only what a server owner may want to tune is in the files. The park geometry (border, arrival point, rest houses) is generated with the terrain (`park/ParkGeometry`, see above), the dimension id is fixed (`cobblemon_safari:safari`), and fine-tuning values with one sensible setting are named constants next to the code that uses them (`FeaturedSpawner`: distances, radius and fallback levels of special spawns; `NativeSpawnRules.SAME_SPECIES_RADIUS`; `SafariBounties.TYPE_MIN/MAX_COUNT`; `SafariEscapeRopeItem.COOLDOWN_SECONDS`; `SafariPoints.MAX_BALANCE`). Fire spread and explosions follow `protection.protectBlocks`, and the capture bonus applies to `loan.ballItem`. Unknown keys are ignored and dropped when the file is rewritten. Each file carries a `configVersion`, written by the mod: it is raised only when a key changes meaning, so a newer version can convert older files. `server.json` is at 3 (2: the split of the species rules between `spawns` and `featured`, see above; 3: the rest house travel wait moved from `escapeRope` to `restHouseTravel`); `shop.json` is at 2 (2: the fossil ticket trade added once to older files) and `client.json` at 1.

## Extension points

- `SafariAccess.requestEntry(...)` + `EntryRequirement`: tickets, fees, NPC gates.
- `SafariSessionManager.addListener(...)`: start, time change, catch and end events (the built-in statistics use it).
- `SafariSessionManager.addTime / setTime`: time items.

## Commands

Players have a single command, `/safari`: their time left (or that they are not hunting), banked time and Safari points. Everything else they do goes through the gates, the NPCs and the wall screens. The NPCs' dialogues call MoLang functions of the player rather than commands (`command/SafariDialogueFunctions`, added to Cobblemon's `PlayerMoLangFunctions.custom`): `q.player.safari_shop()`, `safari_bounties()`, `safari_gate()`, `safari_leave()`, `safari_status()`, `safari_today()` and `safari_balls()` (lists in chat the Poké Balls usable in the Safari, by their item names: `allowedBalls` plus the lent ball when the loan is on; both the warden and the clerk offer it as "Which Poké Balls…?"), the Escape Rope's `safari_rope_can_climb()`, `safari_rope_climb()`, `safari_rope_can_rest_house()` and `safari_rope_rest_house()` (see Escape Rope), and the Safari Guide's `safari_guide_can_<direction>()` and `safari_guide_go_<direction>()` (see Rest houses), and the clerk's mission swap, `safari_bounty_can_swap()`, `safari_bounty_can_swap_<slot>()` and `safari_bounty_swap_<slot>()` (slot 0 to 7, see Bounties), each returning 1 when the action went through; a datapack's dialogues can use them too. A dialogue running a command in the player's name would need a command every player may type; functions are only reachable from dialogues.

Cobblemon stacks a dialogue's answers under its box with no limit, so many answers (the Guide's seven destinations at a large GUI scale) ran off the screen. In any Cobblemon dialogue, the mod's or a datapack's, answers that do not fit scroll (`client/dialogue/DialogueOptionPager`, applied by `mixin/client/ScreenDialogueOptionsMixin` after Minecraft's `Screen.init` and `rebuildWidgets`): as many as fit are shown, with a scroll bar beside them (mouse wheel over the answers, drag the thumb, or click the track to jump a window), one whole answer at a time since Cobblemon draws its answers itself and a half-shown one could not be clipped. Nothing changes while they fit; a horizontal row of answers is left as Cobblemon lays it out. Cobblemon also centres an answer's text on its button whatever its width, so a long answer (a mission with its reward) spilled over both edges: an answer wider than its button minus 6 pixels a side is drawn by the mod instead, the button as Cobblemon draws it and the text shrunk around its centre, down to 55 % (`client/dialogue/DialogueOptionTextFit`, from `mixin/client/DialogueOptionTextFitMixin` at the head of `DialogueOptionWidget.renderWidget`); answers that fit are left to Cobblemon. Answers scrolled out of the window are parked far above the screen but stay visible: Cobblemon's answer takes a click from its last hover state alone, which Minecraft refreshes only on visible widgets, so a hidden answer kept the hover it had when it left and stole the next click (the Guide took players to the wrong house after a scroll, fixed in 1.3.0). The scroll bar, first among the screen's children, also hands a click on the answers to the one under the mouse only.

Operators (level 2) look after players: `/safari info <player>` (the same status for someone else), `/safari enter [targets]` (spends the targets' banked time), `/safari enter <targets> <seconds>` (explicit length for testing), `/safari exit <targets>`, `/safari time add|remove|set <targets> <seconds>`, `/safari credit add <targets> <seconds>`, `/safari points add <targets> <amount>`, `/safari stats <player>`, `/safari top`, `/safari today`, `/safari shop` (opens it anywhere, to check its configuration), `/safari reload`, `/safari reset`, `/safari rotate` (starts a new rotation now, with new specials, bounties, shop Pokémon and purchase limits; it ends at the next scheduled change, so rotations stay aligned on midnight UTC. Counted in the world state, `rotation_offset`, so the rotation index only grows and never reuses an earlier rotation's draw or progress).

## Crafting

Recipes (`data/cobblemon_safari/recipe/`), each unlocked in the recipe book once the player holds a Safari Ticket (leather for the coat rack, `advancement/recipes/`):
- **Safari Wall Screen**: 3 iron ingots on top, then glass pane, Safari Ticket, glass pane, then glass pane, redstone dust, glass pane.
- **Safari Banner**: any banner and a Safari Ticket, shapeless (a banner with patterns is used up too; the picture is chosen once placed).
- **Coat rack**: leather on top, a stick under it, three wooden slabs at the bottom.

The ticket of a recipe can be any Safari Ticket, a long one included. The Escape Rope and the ticket have no recipe: a crafted rope would be taken back as soon as its holder left the Safari, and a crafted ticket would bypass the shop and the chests.

## Safari time, tickets and capture

A Safari Ticket (one item, icon drawn from `tools/assets/safari_ticket_source.png` by `tools/item_icons.py`) adds `ticket.defaultDurationSeconds` (30 min) of Safari time: banked when used outside the park, up to `ticket.maxCreditSeconds` (4 h); used inside, added to the hunt, up to `maxDurationSeconds`. Near the cap a ticket adds only what fits, but never less than `ticket.minAddedSeconds` (20 min): with 3 h 40 banked it adds 20 min, with 3 h 45 it is refused and kept. Tickets are earned two ways. **Trades**: the shop's Tickets tab trades overworld goods for a ticket (by default 16 Apricorns, 24 Berries, 1 evolution stone or 1 fossil, any kind: a cost `item` starting with `#` is an item tag, here Cobblemon's `#cobblemon:apricorns`, `#cobblemon:berries`, `#cobblemon:evolution_stones`, `#cobblemon:fossils`, and any mix of its items counts; the card cycles through them; a player carrying several kinds of a tag is asked which one to give, in a box listing the kinds they carry (`SafariShopBuyPayload` sends the pick, wire version 2): that kind is taken first and the rest, if it is not enough, from the other kinds in the tag's order, and the trade message names what was really taken; without a pick the tag's order alone decides; a tag's name comes from the key `cobblemon_safari.tag.<namespace>.<path>`, else its id), each once per player and rotation; any shop entry can take items (`cost`: list of `item`, `count`, on top of `price`) and be limited (`limitPerRotation`), counts saved per player (`data/cobblemon_safari_shop_limits.dat`, `shop/ShopLimitStore`). **Chests**: `ticketLoot.sources` in the server config lists loot tables (a folder ending in `/` covers all its tables, e.g. `cobblemon:ruins/common/`) with a chance per chest; by default village houses (5 %, savanna 8 %), desert and jungle temples (10 %) and Cobblemon ruins (15 %). Chests inside the Safari never hold tickets (its map, and its ruins, are rebuilt daily). Added by `loot/TicketLoot` through Fabric's `LootTableEvents.MODIFY` and NeoForge's `LootTableLoadEvent`. Entering at a gate spends the bank (one session at most, the rest stays banked); `/safari` shows it and `/safari credit add <players> <seconds>` (op) fills it. Leaving early through the clerk, or dying inside, returns the unused time of a hunt paid with the bank (`ticket.refundUnusedTimeOnLeave`).

Out of battle, inside the Safari, a Safari Ball multiplies the species catch rate by `capture.safariBallOutOfBattleMultiplier` (3 by default), which offsets Cobblemon halving every capture made outside a battle.

While a player is in the Safari, Cobblemon's spawner around them runs `spawns.spawnRateMultiplier` times faster and allows `spawns.pokemonPerChunkMultiplier` times more Pokémon per chunk (2 and 2 by default). It also favours what the player has not caught yet: species they already caught in the Safari spawn with `spawns.caughtSpeciesWeight` (0.4 by default; lower values make caught species feel gone) of their weight, so a new species is two and a half times as likely as a caught one of the same weight (`spawn/NoveltyInfluence`, a Cobblemon `SpawningInfluence` put on the player's spawner by `SafariSpawnBoost`, fed with the Safari statistics). It acts within Cobblemon's rarity buckets: caught species still appear, and a habitat whose species are all caught looks the same as with the setting off. 1 turns it off.

## Safari Gates

Rare overworld structure (`cobblemon_safari:safari_gate`) in plains, meadows, light forests and savannas: a hand-built lodge around the shimmering pane (`structure/safari_gate.nbt`, 27 × 11 × 27: its ground is the bottom layer, as the terrain adaptation expects; it carries air up to 5 blocks over the ground, and up to one block over the highest built block of each column, and leaves the air above out, so the crowns of neighbouring trees overhanging the gate are kept); the Safari Warden stands beside the pane and his Pokémon roam the plaza. Standing Safari Banners line the way in (the Safari crest) and the pond (the lily pond). Placed with `start_height` -1 and `beard_thin`: the beard lines the surrounding terrain up with the template's bottom layer, so that layer must be the ground (foundations under it leave the build standing on a wall). Placed at the `top_layer_modification` step, after trees and plants: its own chunks' trees are generated first and then cut by that air, so none grows on the plaza. What is left of them (a crown with no trunk) is removed once, in game, by `gate/GateClearing` as soon as the whole area around the gate is loaded (tried every half second from the gate's corner block, without the residents' 10 s warm-up, so a player arriving sees the cut crowns for a moment at most) (read a few columns per tick, at most 4096 blocks a tick, each column only up to its heightmap top, so a few ticks in plains and about 15 in a dense forest): groups of logs resting on nothing solid, then natural leaves more than 6 leaves from any log (vanilla's decay rule, without drops). The pane blocks of the template carry `clear_trees:1b` for that, cleared once done; a gate placed by hand never has it. `structure_clearance` (4 chunks) refuses a spot where any other structure set of the world, vanilla or modded, may start a structure within 4 chunks (its placement grid, frequency and biome; `DryJigsawStructure.nearOtherStructure`). `/locate structure cobblemon_safari:safari_gate` finds the nearest one. It only generates on dry, fairly even ground: the structure uses the mod's `cobblemon_safari:dry_jigsaw` type (`worldgen/DryJigsawStructure`), a jigsaw structure that reads the terrain from the noise around its centre (`footprint_radius`) and skips spots with water or more than `max_relief` blocks of relief. The Safari's ruins and towers use it too.

- **Entry**: works like a nether portal (vanilla `Portal` mechanism, same delay game rules, amber overlay; the pane is the amber dust texture slowly turning on itself, drawn by `tools/portal_textures.py`, with the spore blossom's floating spores and falling petals around it). A gate makes no sound: no hum near it, and the nether portal's whoosh is muted when a player steps in (`LocalPlayerPortalSoundMixin`; other portals keep theirs): when the countdown completes, banked time is spent and the player goes in; without time nothing happens but a message on the action bar. The player is moved a step out of the pane just before entering, so the return point is not inside it. The warden's dialogue does the same through `q.player.safari_gate()`, which only works within 10 blocks of a gate. Turn entry off with `gate.allowEntry`.
- **Warden**: a Cobblemon NPC (`data/cobblemon_safari/npcs/safari_warden.json`, dialogue `data/cobblemon_safari/dialogues/safari_warden.json`, skin `assets/cobblemon_safari/textures/npcs/safari_warden.png`, source `tools/assets/safari_warden_skin.png`). Only generated gates have one: every pane block of `structure/safari_gate.nbt` carries `keeps_warden:1b` in its block entity, while a pane placed by hand saves `keeps_warden:0b` (a gate block saved without the flag, as in a structure file, counts as flagged). He stands 2 blocks along the pane and 3 in front of it (first free of the four quadrants), wanders up to 4 blocks from there (a vanilla restriction around his spot, which Cobblemon's wander task honours; `GateResidents.WARDEN_LEASH`), never despawns. He floats and heads back to land in water (Cobblemon's `floats` and `avoids_water` behaviours in his NPC class: a datapack overriding the class must keep them), and he is teleported to his spot when found in water, more than 6 blocks above or below it, or more than 8 blocks away. He never lingers within 2 blocks of the pane (`GateResidents.PANE_CLEARANCE`), where he would be hard to click without stepping into the portal: the gate checks it every second and walks him back to his spot. His entry and his shop accept any player within 6 blocks of him, not only those within 10 blocks of a gate block (`SafariGate.isNearGate`): talking to him is being at the gate, even when he stands at the edge of his leash. Only wardens spawned by a gate carry his tag, so a Safari Warden NPC placed by hand elsewhere does not open the way. He and his Pokémon take no damage from anything but `/kill` and the void, and whoever is removed comes back on the gate's next check (every 5 s while a player is within 48 blocks), like the rest house clerk: a generated gate always gets its warden back. `gate.spawnWarden` turns him off.
- **Roaming Pokémon**: one per entry of `gate.companions` (Cobblemon properties), within `gate.roamRadius` blocks. Uncatchable, invulnerable, battles against them are cancelled. They despawn when nobody is around and come back when a player is within 48 blocks.
- **Frequency**: `data/cobblemon_safari/worldgen/structure_set/safari_gate.json` (spacing 48 chunks, separation 20). Override it in a datapack to change it, or point the biome tag `has_structure/safari_gate` to nothing to disable the structure. Only new chunks are affected.
- **Operators** can build their own gate with the creative Safari Gate block (unbreakable in survival): the roaming Pokémon settle at any gate up to 8 blocks wide; no warden comes with it. To give one a warden anyway: `/data merge block <x> <y> <z> {keeps_warden:1b}` on any of its pane blocks.
- The template is built by hand in game and saved with a structure block, its bottom layer being the ground (nothing under it; a pond is at most one block deep) and with the air above the clearing height turned to structure void; its pane blocks carry `keeps_warden:1b` and `clear_trees:1b` (added with a typed NBT edit: a gate re-saved in game has lost `clear_trees` once it ticked, put it back in the saved file with an NBT editor, on every `cobblemon_safari:safari_gate` block entity; setting it in game with `/data` would make the gate clear its surroundings within seconds, before the save). `tools/safari_gate_assets.py` writes the warden's skin (`--structure` also writes a procedurally generated lodge, overwriting the hand-built one).
- **Logging in** inside the Safari without a session sends the player out, creative and spectator included (the `exemptCreativeAndSpectator` exemption only applies to players already connected). A player who gets in another way without a session (a waystone, another mod's teleport, a command) is sent back within a second to where they last stood outside, whatever their banked time: a hunt only starts at a gate (or with an operator's `/safari enter`).
- **Spawning gamerule**: with Cobblemon's `doPokemonSpawning` set to false, the Safari spawns no Pokémon at all, specials included.

## Safari points and the shop

Every capture inside the Safari earns Safari points (`points` section: by the species' rarity in the Safari, plus a bonus for today's specials and for shinies: 10, 20, 50 or 120 points, +50 for a special, +250 for a shiny), a currency separate from Safari time. With the bounties (90 to 250 points a mission by default, 200 once all are done) the defaults give about 600 points per hour of active hunting, enough for a first-stage Pokémon of the shop in about a quarter of an hour. Points always show with their logo, a gold coin glyph of the mod's font (`assets/cobblemon_safari/font/icons.json`, `points/SafariPointsIcon`), in chat, the shop and on the board.

Two shopkeepers open the shop: the Safari Warden at every Safari Gate (he holds a Safari Ball) and the Safari Clerk behind the counter of every rest house (NPC class `safari_clerk`, put back automatically when a loaded house has none, `structure/RestHouseStaff`). The clerk also lists the current missions and, after a confirmation, lets a hunter leave the Safari early (`q.player.safari_leave()`), which also walks out a player standing in the Safari without a game (a creative or spectator player exempt from the rules), to the world spawn. Their dialogue opens a prize-counter screen over a pixel-art safari landscape that follows the time of day in game: morning, dusk or night (`textures/gui/shop_background_<scene>.png`, drawn by `tools/gui_assets.py`), each with a trainer and original creatures of the mod: tabs (by default Pokémon, Items, Poké Balls, Cooking and Tickets), then cards with gently swaying animated Cobblemon models or enlarged items, 8 per page; a name too long for its card scrolls back and forth.

`config/cobblemon_safari/shop.json` lists `tabs` (`id`, optional `name` shown as is, `icon` item id; built-in ids `pokemon`, `items`, `balls`, `cooking`, `tickets` have translated names) and `entries`: items (`"type": "item"`, `item`, `count`) and Pokémon (`"type": "pokemon"`, Cobblemon properties in `pokemon`), each with a `price` in points and a `tab`; fields of the other type are left out of the file. `cooldownHours` makes a player wait that many hours (real time, so the wait goes on while they or the server are offline) before buying an entry again, whatever the rotation; the card then shows the wait left and the Buy button is greyed. By default it serves the free Safari Balls: 30 `cobblemon:safari_ball` for 0 points, `cooldownHours` 24, first in the balls tab; a free entry shows "Free" and a "Take" button. Purchase times are saved per player with the limits (`data/cobblemon_safari_shop_limits.dat`). A Pokémon entry may set `"price": "auto"` instead of a number: it is then priced like the random Pokémon (its rarity in the Safari times its evolution stage, from the `rotating` base prices), recomputed whenever the shop is listed, so it follows those prices (`config/ShopPriceAdapter`, resolved in `SafariShop.catalogue`). An item entry with `"auto"` is removed (nothing to price it from), and any other text makes the file unreadable until fixed (the log names the value). An entry without a valid tab goes to the one matching its type (ids ending in `_ball` go to `balls`); a tab without entries is hidden. Select a card, then press Buy; the server re-checks everything (points, being at a gate or in a rest house, unchanged price); a Pokémon goes to the party, or the PC when it is full. The NPCs open it at a gate or in a rest house (`/safari shop` anywhere for operators), `/safari` shows the balance, `/safari points add <players> <amount>` (op) gives points. `/safari reload` re-reads the shop file.

By default the fixed entries sell a single Pokémon, a level 45 Tauros for 200 points, plus items and Poké Balls (item cards show the stack size in the corner), and in the Cooking tab what the rest houses' Poké Pot needs: Cobblemon's Hearty Grains, Vivichoke, Medicinal Leek, Tasty Tail, Galarica Nuts, Pep-Up Flowers and Energy Roots, with honey, sugar and milk. When the tabs do not fit their row, they first tighten (smaller icon and padding, the row using the whole box width), then drop their icons and keep their names, and if even that is too wide the row scrolls with arrows at both ends; no tab is ever hidden. After the fixed entries, the shop sells `rotating.count` random Pokémon (15 by default) that change with each rotation of the specials, the same for every player (`shop/RotatingShop`, deterministic like the specials). They are drawn among the species that spawn naturally in the Safari, with the rarities listed in `rotating.rarities` (common, uncommon and rare by default; base prices 250, 500, 500 and 2500 by default, the rare one kept close to the uncommon one so that rarity does not outweigh strength), sold at the lowest level they spawn at in the Safari (so an evolved Pokémon is never sold below its evolution level), in `rotating.tab`. Their price is the base price of their rarity (`commonPrice`, `uncommonPrice`, `rarePrice`, `ultraRarePrice`) times a multiplier for their evolution stage (`RotatingShop.STAGE_MULTIPLIERS`: ×1 first stage, ×2 second, ×3 third; a species alone in its line counts as a second stage), rounded to 50: a spawn bucket says how often a Pokémon appears, not how strong it is, and Cobblemon files many final evolutions as common. A Pokémon the fixed entries already sell is never drawn: same species and same form (compared by Cobblemon's Showdown id, e.g. `vulpixalola`), whatever its level, so no Pokémon is listed twice, while another form of the species (a plain Vulpix next to a fixed Alolan one) still can be. `excludeSpecials` leaves out the current specials, `allowSpecialSpecies` allows legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox species, `excludedSpecies` lists species never drawn, `rotating.enabled` turns them off. The items and balls tabs work the same way (`rotatingItems`, `rotatingBalls`, `shop/RotatingItems`): each rotation draws `count` items (8 and 6 by default) among those its `prices` rules cover, plus its `always` items (Safari Balls by default), leaving out `excluded` ids and `#tags` and anything the fixed entries already sell. Each rule names an item or an item `#tag`, the stack sold and its price; the first rule matching an item wins, so specific items go before their tags. The defaults price Cobblemon's own tags (medicine, candies, mints, mochis, battle items, gems, evolution stones and items, held items; balls by tier) against about 600 points per hour of hunting, and ban the Ability Patch, Sinistcha's teapots and teacups, and the vanilla bone and snowball that Cobblemon's held-item tag contains. A purchase made with a screen opened before a new draw is refused ("the shop changed"), so nobody buys a Pokémon they never saw.

## Bounties (missions of the rotation)

With each rotation of the specials, `bounties.count` missions are drawn (deterministically, from the rotation and the world's salt, so they survive restarts), each of a kind drawn by weight among `bounties.allowedKinds` (`bounty/BountyKind`: species, type, special, habitat, any, different species, out of battle, night, fully evolved, level, hidden ability, shiny), different kinds first. They are the same for every player. Their targets come from `bounty/BountyPool`, the species whose natural spawns in the surface habitats pass `bounties.speciesFilter`: it reads Cobblemon's spawn pool itself, spawn by spawn and condition by condition, because the habitat index of the specials merges a species' spawns and can no longer tell a fished, cave, nocturnal or rainy spawn from a plain one (built on demand, kept until the filter or the data changes). A kind that cannot be met this rotation is skipped. Out of battle is told from the catch rate event, which Cobblemon fires for every throw (the ball is remembered with whether its target was in a battle); night is Cobblemon's spawn night (12542 to 23459); the habitat is the biome where the ball lies. Each mission pays its reward in Safari points once per player, plus `bounties.allDoneBonus` when all are done. The clerk lists them in chat, with the player's progress (the wall screens' BOUNTIES page shows them too), and swaps one for `bounties.swap.cost` points, `perRotation` times per rotation: the replacement is drawn for that player alone (rotation, salt, player, slot and swap number), unlike any of their current missions and of another kind when possible, and starts from zero (dialogue texts `cobblemon_safari:bounty_mission` and `cobblemon_safari:bounty_swap_prompt`, `bounty/BountyDialogueTexts`). Progress, the species counted by a "different species" mission and the swapped missions are saved per player and per rotation (`data/cobblemon_safari_bounties.dat`, format 2): unfinished missions are lost when the rotation changes.

## Escape Rope

Its texture is a 16 × 16 pixel-art coil with a Poké Ball handle, made by `tools/escape_rope_icon.py` from the sprite in `tools/assets/safari_escape_rope_source.png` (a screenshot: the script reads one colour per sprite pixel and snaps it to a small palette). Lent on entry (`escapeRope.enabled`) and taken back on exit, never worn out. With a full inventory it is dropped at the player's feet, and the entry message says so. A player who lost or dropped it takes another, free, from the clerk of any rest house: the shops inside the Safari list it last in the items tab (built in memory, not in `shop.json`), and refuse it to a player who already carries one ("Carried" on the card). The gate shops do not list it, since a rope outside the Safari is taken back within a second. Used in the Safari, it opens a small Cobblemon dialogue (`data/cobblemon_safari/dialogues/escape_rope.json`, actions in `item/EscapeRopeActions`): "Climb to the open air", shown only when the player's eyes are under cover (a cave, an overhang, a canopy), "Go to the nearest rest house", and "Put it away". Climbing does not go straight up: the surface of a column ignores leaves, so under a tree it is the top of the trunk, inside the crown, where players got stuck. `SafariTeleporter.findStandingSpotNear` searches ring by ring up to 12 blocks around the player for ground that is solid, dry, neither leaves nor a trunk top nor harmful (magma, cactus, campfires), with two blocks free of collision, liquid and harm (fire, berry bushes, powder snow, cobwebs, wither roses) above it, outside the rest houses and inside the park; the nearest one under the open sky wins, else the nearest one under cover, else the rope says there is no safe spot. The park boundary's pull-back uses the same search, falling back to the bare column. The rest house option (`restHouseTravel.ropeEnabled`) is a trip like the Safari Guide's (see Rest houses): to the nearest house, built or not, with the flight cinematic (taking off where the rope was used, after a climb to the open air from a cave) or its pixel-art scene, in front of its steps facing the door; it works in the open air too, and shares the guide's wait. Each teleport puts the rope on a 5 s cooldown. Nothing works during a battle or outside the Safari, and the actions check that again when chosen, since the dialogue may stay open. Without the dialogue file (removed by a datapack) the rope only climbs out. Any rope found outside the Safari is removed.

## Daily map reset

At the first server start of each new day (`reset.hour`, `reset.timeZone`), the Safari dimension folder is deleted before the levels load and regenerated with a new terrain seed (a salt mixed into the world seed for that dimension only, `ChunkMapMixin`). Rest houses are rebuilt on the new map; players who logged out inside are moved to the arrival point. Sessions, statistics and banked time are kept. State: `<world>/cobblemon_safari_map.json`. `/safari reset` (op) forces a reset at the next start. Turn it off with `reset.enabled`.
