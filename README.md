# Cobblemon Safari

A Safari Zone for Cobblemon (Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric + NeoForge): a procedural park dimension, timed hunts, and a configurable Poké Ball allow-list enforced server-side.

Every item of the mod (ticket, Escape Rope, gate, wall screen, decor) is in its own creative tab, "Cobblemon Safari" (`item/SafariCreativeTab`, registered by each loader: `FabricItemGroup` on Fabric, the creative tab registry event on NeoForge).

## Layout

| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| `common` | All gameplay logic, payloads, HUD, worldgen data |
| `fabric` / `neoforge` | Thin bridges from loader events to `SafariServerEvents` |
| `tools/worldgen` | Generator of every worldgen JSON file (do not hand-edit its output) |
| `example/safari-spawns-datapack` | Example Cobblemon spawns targeting the Safari tags |

## Worldgen

The Safari is a single island (shape radius 512 blocks, land to about 435, centred on 0,0) in an endless turquoise sea, enclosed by its own square world border. A summit sits at the centre, ringed by rocky hills and a volcano. Around it, 14 habitats are laid out as sectors from the rocky ring to the coast, each with a whole sector of its own: grassland (north, where players arrive), savanna, dunes, badlands, jungle, marsh, mushroom grove, flower meadow, woodland, deep forest, taiga, snowfield, glacier, rocky hills. Caves (and lush caves in the south) run underneath.

The layout relies on one Java density function type, `cobblemon_safari:island_coordinate`, which exposes distance and direction from the island centre; everything else is data. Sector borders are wobbled by noise (`CLIMATE_NOISE`), so no two maps look alike and no border is a straight line, while the smallest sector of any map keeps about three quarters of its nominal size.

The sky palette (turquoise zenith, golden horizon, coral sunsets, low clouds) comes from biome colours plus `SafariSkyEffects` on the client.

Run `python3 tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py` after changing biomes, tags, island size or terrain tuning. It also writes `park/ParkGeometry.java` (park centre, border half-size, arrival point and spread, rest house anchors), so the border, the arrival and the rest houses always match the generated terrain: there is nothing to copy into a config file. It only rewrites the folders it owns; the Safari Gate's structure files are left alone.

## Protection

Inside the Safari, players take no damage (except /kill and the void), cannot change the terrain (breaking, placing, buckets, block-transforming tools), and fire never spreads. Creative and spectator players are exempt from the terrain rules when `exemptCreativeAndSpectator` is on. Each rule has a switch under `protection` in the server config.

## Biome tags (spawn contract)

Habitat tags: `safari_plains`, `safari_savanna`, `safari_desert`, `safari_badlands`, `safari_jungle`, `safari_swamp`, `safari_meadow`, `safari_forest`, `safari_deep_forest`, `safari_taiga`, `safari_snow`, `safari_ice`, `safari_rocky`, `safari_mushroom`, `safari_mountain`, `safari_volcanic`, `safari_lake`, `safari_river`, `safari_beach`, `safari_lush_cave`.
Aggregates: `safari_cave`, `safari_grassland`, `safari_wooded`, `safari_water`, `safari_wetland`, `safari_arid`, `safari_frozen`, `safari_highland`, `is_safari`.

Use them in Cobblemon spawn files as `"biomes": ["#cobblemon_safari:safari_forest"]` to add Pokémon to the park only.

Each Safari biome is also bridged into the matching Cobblemon habitat tags (`data/cobblemon/tags/worldgen/biome`, generated from `COBBLEMON_TAG_BRIDGE`), so Cobblemon's natural spawns and spawn datapacks populate the park on their own. The snowfield also takes Cobblemon's snowy forest and snowy taiga tags, and the glacier its frozen ocean tag: with only `is_snowy` and `is_tundra`, the snowfield had about six species one could meet without bait (Swinub, Stantler, Sentret and Cetoddle only come to a Poké Snack in Cobblemon). The volcano also takes the badlands tag, the mushroom grove the magical one and the flower meadow the plains one, so every surface habitat has 30 to 130 species one can meet without bait. Catch-all tags such as `#cobblemon:is_overworld` are left out of surface habitats on purpose so habitats stay distinct; the underground cavern alone takes `is_overworld`, because Cobblemon spawns its cave Pokémon (Zubat, Geodude, Onix, Gible... about 126 species) through it with underground conditions, exactly as in any cave of a normal Cobblemon world. Caves are not surface habitats (`SafariBiomes.SURFACE_HABITATS`), so this changes nothing for the specials, the shop or the bounties. `spawns` in the config filters what comes in: legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox Pokémon each have a section (`spawns.legendary`, `mythical`, `ultraBeast`, `paradox`) with `allowSpawn` (refused by default, except paradox Pokémon, allowed) and `maxSpawnPerPokemon`, the most Pokémon of each of their species per rotation of the specials, whether they came as a special or through Cobblemon's own spawns (1 by default, 0 for no cap, which is the paradox default). A slot is held by each one caught in the Safari during the rotation and by each one still roaming; one that leaves uncaught (despawned, fled, knocked out) gives its slot back within about ten seconds, so a missed Rayquaza can come back for another player (`spawn/SpecialSpawnCounter`, which sweeps the roaming ones every 5 s; saved in `data/cobblemon_safari_special_spawns.dat`). Before config version 21 these were the flags `allowLegendary`, `allowMythical`, `allowUltraBeast` and `allowParadox`, moved into the sections on the first start. Excluded species never appear, and a same-species cap keeps one Pokémon from flooding a habitat.

## Special Pokémon of the day

Every rotation (4 h by default, `featured.rotationMinutes`, aligned on midnight UTC: 0, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 h UTC), the mod draws a few uncommon, rare and ultra-rare species (by default 1, 2 and 3: `featured.uncommonSlots`, `rareSlots`, `ultraRareSlots`) from what is actually installed: Cobblemon's spawn data decides each species' habitat and rarity, and species without a natural spawn get a habitat from their types. The draw is deterministic per world and rotation, mixes rarities, and prefers different habitats. A new rotation, natural or forced with `/safari rotate`, never brings back a special of the rotation before (unless nothing else can fill a slot): the last specials drawn and the ones their draw left out are kept in the world state (`specials`, `specials_excluded`), so a restart or reload redraws the same list. Starter lines (any species whose first stage carries Cobblemon's `starter` label: Cyndaquil, Quilava and Typhlosion alike) and the Eevee line (Eevee and its eight evolutions) get a slot only one time in four between them (`FeaturedRotation.CROWDING_SLOT_CHANCE`, `SpeciesRules.isCrowdingLine`) when other species of the same rarity are available: Cobblemon files most starters as ultra-rare, where the 27 lines would otherwise take most of the slots, and the eeveelutions land together in one rarity too. The mod spawns these specials itself, near players and only in their habitat, with caps so none takes over. The list is shown by the wall screens, by `/safari today`, and on entry.

### Safari Wall Screen

`cobblemon_safari:safari_wall_screen`: a live display on a panel held off the wall by one arm; up to 3 side by side merge, with the arm in the middle. Right-click with an empty hand opens its settings (who may: `block/WallScreenSettings`): what it shows (specials, the viewer's stats, missions) and its tilt, a slider from -30° (facing the ceiling) to +30° (facing the floor) by 5°. The tilt is the block state `tilt` (0 to 6 steps) plus `upward`, so screens placed before negative tilts existed keep their angle.

The FEATURED page shows the current specials with their animated Cobblemon models, rarity and habitat (scrolling when too long), and a progress bar: a viewer with a session sees their own hunting time drain, anyone else sees the time until the next rotation. Specials are split over pages when they do not fit. Everything is drawn client side from data the server pushes (`SafariFeaturedPayload`), so a screen works anywhere, in any dimension, without configuration; the picture is painted by `client/board/SafariScreenPainter`. Behind the panels, the screen shows its own pixel-art landscape (a watering hole at the foot of the volcano, `textures/gui/board_background_<light>.png`, drawn by `tools/gui_assets.py`) in the light of the time of day in game (morning, dusk or night), dimmed and cropped around the horizon; `board.backgroundImage` turns it off (plain `board.colors.background` colour) and `board.panelOpacity` (0 to 1, default 0.72) sets how much the title band, cells and bars hide it. `board.useSprites` chooses how the Pokémon are drawn: `false` (default, turning 3D Cobblemon models) or `true`, the mod's own pixel sprites, `assets/cobblemon_safari/textures/pokemon_sprites/<species>[_<form>][_shiny].png` (`client/board/PokemonSprites`), which a resource pack can override with the same names; a Pokémon without a sprite keeps its model. `python3 tools/pokemon_sprites_pack.py <folder> --mod` installs them from a folder laid out as `<dex>_<name>/<name>[_<form>][_shiny].png` (or `<folder> <pack.zip>` makes a resource pack instead). Every colour of the screen can be changed in `config/cobblemon_safari/client.json` (`board.colors` section, `#RRGGBB` or `#AARRGGBB`; a colour with its own alpha keeps it over the landscape). The client config section keeps its old name, `board`, from the Safari Board screens that were removed (their block, the frame and the entrance house's board are gone; a world that had some finds air in their place). The rotation length is `featured.rotationMinutes` in `config/cobblemon_safari/server.json`.

## Decor

Furniture for rest houses and gates, in the mod's creative tab (`decor/SafariDecor`). Textures are drawn by `tools/decor_textures.py`, models, block states, loot tables and the axe tag by `tools/decor_assets.py`.

- **Safari Banner** (`safari_banner`, `safari_wall_banner` on a wall; one item places both, like vanilla banners): a pole, a bar and a cloth swaying like a vanilla banner's, same size, drawn by `client/banner/SafariBannerRenderer`. The cloth shows a picture chosen with right-click and an empty hand (`client/banner/BannerDesignScreen`, a grid of thumbnails with a scrollbar when they overflow, same rights as the wall screens, `decor/BannerDesigns`), saved in the block entity and sent to clients in its update packet. A picture is any texture under `textures/block/banner/`, in any namespace (32 × 64 for the shipped ones, any 1:2 size works): vanilla's block atlas takes everything under `block/`, so a resource pack adds pictures by dropping PNGs there, and a `.png.mcmeta` animates one (frames stacked vertically, `"width"` and `"height"` giving the frame size, as the animated ones shipped do: `river_waves`, `fireflies`, `waterfall`, `campfire`, `rainstorm`, `windy_grass`, `lily_pond`, `shooting_stars`, `sun_rays`; a picture may pick its own frame count and frame time). Names come from `cobblemon_safari.banner_design.<namespace>.<file>`, else from the file name. Pictures are drawn by `tools/banner_textures.py`. A broken banner drops a plain banner: the picture is chosen again once placed.
- **Coat rack** (`coat_rack`): two blocks tall, a ranger's jacket on its front hook and a ranger's hat on top; a dye recolours the hat (block state `hat`, 16 colours, brown when placed; same rights as the wall screens). Built like vanilla's tall plants; only the lower half has loot, so breaking either half drops one rack.

## Rest houses

11 × 11 Pokémon Centers built once per map, always (they are the only place to heal inside the park), door toward the island centre (`structure/RestHouseBuilder`). There are four, one per anchor of `park/ParkGeometry.REST_HOUSES`, generated with the terrain by `tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py` (`ENTRANCE_HOUSE_NORTH_OF_ARRIVAL`, `REST_HOUSE_RING`): the entrance just north of the arrival point, then one house east, south and west, on the arrival's ring. They are not in the server config, so they always match the island. Each anchor is a starting point: `structure/RestHouseSites` reads the terrain the generator will produce (without generating chunks) on a grid around it and builds at the flattest dry spot inside the park, at least 64 blocks from the other houses and clear of the ruin and tower spots; the entrance stays within 32 blocks of its anchor, the others within 64. Where each house went is saved in the world state. Inside: a Cobblemon Healing Machine on the counter that never runs out of charge, a Cobblemon PC, a cooking corner with a Poké Pot on a Cobblemon campfire, and seats. Outside: a stone plaza with lanterns and flower beds. Houses are built when a map is generated, so a layout change shows after the next map reset. Spawns and arrivals keep clear of the whole footprint, plaza included (`RestHouses.FOOTPRINT_REACH`).

In the Safari, right-clicking a Cobblemon station (PC, Healing Machine, campfire) always works whatever the player holds, and a crouching click on a campfire is refused so the Poké Pot stays.

## Ruins and towers

Every map has Cobblemon ruins and Gimmighoul towers, through the Safari's own structures (`worldgen/structure/safari_ruins/`, written by `tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py`, `SAFARI_STRUCTURES`) that reuse Cobblemon's template pools: towers in six looks (temperate, lush, rooted, deserted, sunscorched, frozen) and ruins standing above ground (henges, toppled pillars, shaded ruins, a desert town centre, a frozen altar, an overgrown trial), each limited to the habitats it suits. Two structure sets spread them: a tower candidate every 320 blocks and a ruin candidate every 224 blocks, so a map has several of each in practice. They are generated with the terrain, so they blend in like in the overworld, and change with the daily map.

## Ambience

The Safari has its own music: six original tracks composed and rendered by `tools/music/compose_safari_music.py` (pure synthesis, no samples, deterministic) into `assets/cobblemon_safari/sounds/music/`. Each biome plays the track of its zone (`HABITAT_SOUNDS` in `tools/worldgen/generate_worldgen.py`): `plains` (grassland, savanna, flower meadow, water), `forest` (woodland, deep forest, taiga, jungle, marsh, mushroom grove), `arid` (dunes, badlands, rocky hills, volcanic), `snow` (snowfield, glacier, peaks) and `caves`. Vanilla music is never heard in the Safari. Tracks are 1 to 3 minutes apart, and crossing into another zone lets the current track finish.

Inside a rest house the `center` theme starts at once (`client/music/SafariMusic`, through `MinecraftMusicMixin` on `Minecraft.getSituationalMusic`); it loops while the player stays inside. The client knows where the houses are from the rules payload. Creative players hear the zone music too, instead of vanilla's creative music.

The ambient sound of each biome is the cry of a Pokémon one can actually meet there (common spawns of the habitat's bridged tags in Cobblemon 1.8.0, no bait needed), a few species per biome, played quietly at random (`additions_sound`, chance 0.004 per tick, about once every 12 seconds). These `ambient.<biome>` events point at Cobblemon's own cry files.

To change the music: edit `THEMES` in the composer, run it, then run `generate_worldgen.py` (which checks every zone has its track and rewrites `sounds.json` and the biomes).

## Protection

Inside the Safari, by default: players take no damage (except /kill and the void), cannot break or place blocks or use block-changing tools, fire never spreads, and explosions destroy no block. Creative and spectator players are exempt from the terrain rules. Blocks listed in `protection.placeableBlocks` (by default Cobblemon's Poké Cake and Poké Snack) can still be placed and picked up. Breaking a protected block is refused only when it would break, so mining shows its cracks and the warning appears once. Each rule can be switched off in the `protection` section of the config.

## Safari Game loop

- **Entry**: always paid with banked Safari time, which only tickets (or `/safari credit add`) provide: walk into a Safari Gate, talk to its warden, `/safari start`, `/safari enter` (op), or any custom access method through `SafariAccess`. A ticket can carry its own duration: `/give @p cobblemon_safari:safari_ticket[custom_data={duration_seconds:600}]`. Used inside, a ticket extends the running session.
- **Loaned Safari Balls**: 30 by default (`loan.count`), handed out on entry and taken back on exit. They only work inside the Safari and are reclaimed from any player found outside with one.
- **HUD**: the time left only, under Cobblemon's Safari Ball that rocks gently (faster in the last `criticalTimeSeconds`), left of the hotbar by default (`hudAnchor`, `hudOffsetX/Y` place the ball's centre, in `config/cobblemon_safari/client.json`).
- **Recap** at the end of each game, and lifetime records: `/safari stats [player]`, `/safari top`.

## Configuration files

Everything lives in `config/cobblemon_safari/`: `server.json` (rules of the Safari, server side), `shop.json` (the shop) and `client.json` (each player's display preferences). Files from older versions, directly in `config/` (`cobblemon_safari.json`, `cobblemon_safari_shop.json`, `cobblemon_safari-client.json`), are moved into the folder on the next start, settings kept. `/safari reload` re-reads `server.json` and `shop.json`. A file that cannot be parsed (a typo, a missing brace) is never overwritten: the mod runs on the defaults and says so in the log until the file is fixed, even if those defaults would otherwise be saved after a correction (`JsonConfigFile.unreadable`).

Only what a server owner may want to tune is in the files. The park geometry (border, arrival point, rest houses) is generated with the terrain (`park/ParkGeometry`, see above), the dimension id is fixed (`cobblemon_safari:safari`), and fine-tuning values with one sensible setting are named constants next to the code that uses them (`FeaturedSpawner`: distances, radius and fallback levels of special spawns; `NativeSpawnRules.SAME_SPECIES_RADIUS`; `SafariBounties.TYPE_MIN/MAX_COUNT`; `SafariEscapeRopeItem.COOLDOWN_SECONDS`; `SafariPoints.MAX_BALANCE`). Fire spread and explosions follow `protection.protectBlocks`, and the capture bonus applies to `loan.ballItem`. Keys of removed settings found in an older file are ignored and dropped when the file is rewritten; client files of version 5 or older get their board colours moved into `board.colors` and `pokemonDisplay` turned into `useSprites`.

## Extension points

- `SafariAccess.requestEntry(...)` + `EntryRequirement`: tickets, fees, NPC gates.
- `SafariSessionManager.addListener(...)`: start, time change, catch and end events (the built-in statistics use it).
- `SafariSessionManager.addTime / setTime`: time items.

## Commands

`/safari enter [targets]` (op, spends the targets' banked time), `/safari enter <targets> <seconds>` (op, explicit length for testing), `/safari gate`, `/safari exit <targets>`, `/safari leave`, `/safari time`, `/safari time add|remove|set <targets> <seconds>`, `/safari reload`, `/safari stats [player]`, `/safari top`, `/safari today`, `/safari rotate` (op: starts a new rotation now, with new specials, bounties, shop Pokémon and purchase limits; it ends at the next scheduled change, so rotations stay aligned on midnight UTC. Counted in the world state, `rotation_offset`, so the rotation index only grows and never reuses an earlier rotation's draw or progress).

## Safari time, tickets and capture

A Safari Ticket (one item, icon drawn from `tools/assets/safari_ticket_source.png` by `tools/item_icons.py`) adds `ticket.defaultDurationSeconds` (30 min) of Safari time: banked when used outside the park, up to `ticket.maxCreditSeconds` (4 h); used inside, added to the hunt, up to `maxDurationSeconds`. Near the cap a ticket adds only what fits, but never less than `ticket.minAddedSeconds` (20 min): with 3 h 40 banked it adds 20 min, with 3 h 45 it is refused and kept. The 15 min, 30 min and 1 h tickets of earlier versions no longer exist. Tickets are earned two ways. **Trades**: the shop's Tickets tab trades overworld goods for a ticket (by default 16 Apricorns, 24 Berries or 1 evolution stone, any kind: a cost `item` starting with `#` is an item tag, here Cobblemon's `#cobblemon:apricorns`, `#cobblemon:berries`, `#cobblemon:evolution_stones`, and any mix of its items counts; the card cycles through them; a tag's name comes from the key `cobblemon_safari.tag.<namespace>.<path>`, else its id), each once per player and rotation; any shop entry can take items (`cost`: list of `item`, `count`, on top of `price`) and be limited (`limitPerRotation`), counts saved per player (`data/cobblemon_safari_shop_limits.dat`, `shop/ShopLimitStore`). **Chests**: `ticketLoot.sources` in the server config lists loot tables (a folder ending in `/` covers all its tables, e.g. `cobblemon:ruins/common/`) with a chance per chest; by default village houses (5 %, savanna 8 %), desert and jungle temples (10 %) and Cobblemon ruins (15 %). Chests inside the Safari never hold tickets (its map, and its ruins, are rebuilt daily). Added by `loot/TicketLoot` through Fabric's `LootTableEvents.MODIFY` and NeoForge's `LootTableLoadEvent`. `/safari start` enters by spending the bank (one session at most, the rest stays banked; disable with `ticket.allowStartCommand` once a gate exists), `/safari credit` shows it and `/safari credit add <players> <seconds>` (op) fills it. Leaving early with `/safari leave` (or through the clerk), or dying inside, returns the unused time of a hunt paid with the bank (`ticket.refundUnusedTimeOnLeave`).

Out of battle, inside the Safari, a Safari Ball multiplies the species catch rate by `capture.safariBallOutOfBattleMultiplier` (3 by default), which offsets Cobblemon halving every capture made outside a battle.

While a player is in the Safari, Cobblemon's spawner around them runs `spawns.spawnRateMultiplier` times faster and allows `spawns.pokemonPerChunkMultiplier` times more Pokémon per chunk (2 and 2 by default). It also favours what the player has not caught yet: species they already caught in the Safari spawn with `spawns.caughtSpeciesWeight` (0.4 by default; 0.25 before version 20, raised so caught species do not feel gone) of their weight, so a new species is two and a half times as likely as a caught one of the same weight (`spawn/NoveltyInfluence`, a Cobblemon `SpawningInfluence` put on the player's spawner by `SafariSpawnBoost`, fed with the Safari statistics). It acts within Cobblemon's rarity buckets: caught species still appear, and a habitat whose species are all caught looks the same as before. 1 turns it off.

## Safari Gates

Rare overworld structure (`cobblemon_safari:safari_gate`) in plains, meadows, light forests and savannas: a hand-built lodge around the shimmering pane (`structure/safari_gate.nbt`, 27 × 11 × 27: its ground is the bottom layer, as the terrain adaptation expects; it carries air up to 5 blocks over the ground, and up to one block over the highest built block of each column, and leaves the air above out, so the crowns of neighbouring trees overhanging the gate are kept); the Safari Warden stands beside the pane and his Pokémon roam the plaza. Placed with `start_height` -1 and `beard_thin`: the beard lines the surrounding terrain up with the template's bottom layer, so that layer must be the ground (foundations under it leave the build standing on a wall). Placed at the `top_layer_modification` step, after trees and plants: its own chunks' trees are generated first and then cut by that air, so none grows on the plaza. What is left of them (a crown with no trunk) is removed once, in game, by `gate/GateClearing` the first time the gate's corner block ticks with the whole area loaded (read a few columns per tick, at most 4096 blocks a tick, each column only up to its heightmap top, so a few ticks in plains and about 15 in a dense forest): groups of logs resting on nothing solid, then natural leaves more than 6 leaves from any log (vanilla's decay rule, without drops). The pane blocks of the template carry `clear_trees:1b` for that, cleared once done; a gate placed by hand never has it. `structure_clearance` (4 chunks) refuses a spot where any other structure set of the world, vanilla or modded, may start a structure within 4 chunks (its placement grid, frequency and biome; `DryJigsawStructure.nearOtherStructure`). `/locate structure cobblemon_safari:safari_gate` finds the nearest one. It only generates on dry, fairly even ground: the structure uses the mod's `cobblemon_safari:dry_jigsaw` type (`worldgen/DryJigsawStructure`), a jigsaw structure that reads the terrain from the noise around its centre (`footprint_radius`) and skips spots with water or more than `max_relief` blocks of relief. The Safari's ruins and towers use it too.

- **Entry**: works like a nether portal (vanilla `Portal` mechanism, same delay game rules, amber overlay; the pane is the amber dust texture slowly turning on itself, drawn by `tools/portal_textures.py`, with the spore blossom's floating spores and falling petals around it). A gate makes no sound: no hum near it, and the nether portal's whoosh is muted when a player steps in (`LocalPlayerPortalSoundMixin`; other portals keep theirs): when the countdown completes, banked time is spent and the player goes in; without time nothing happens but a message on the action bar. The player is moved a step out of the pane just before entering, so the return point is not inside it. The warden's dialogue does the same through `/safari gate`, which only works within 10 blocks of a gate. Turn entry off with `gate.allowEntry`.
- **Warden**: a Cobblemon NPC (`data/cobblemon_safari/npcs/safari_warden.json`, dialogue `data/cobblemon_safari/dialogues/safari_warden.json`, skin `assets/cobblemon_safari/textures/npcs/safari_warden.png`, source `tools/assets/safari_warden_skin.png`). Only generated gates have one: every pane block of `structure/safari_gate.nbt` carries `keeps_warden:1b` in its block entity, while a pane placed by hand saves `keeps_warden:0b` (gate blocks saved before this flag existed count as flagged, so existing gates keep theirs). He stands 2 blocks along the pane and 3 in front of it (first free of the four quadrants), wanders up to 4 blocks from there, never despawns. He and his Pokémon take no damage from anything but `/kill` and the void, and whoever is removed comes back on the gate's next check (every 5 s while a player is within 48 blocks), like the rest house clerk: a generated gate always gets its warden back. `gate.spawnWarden` turns him off.
- **Roaming Pokémon**: one per entry of `gate.companions` (Cobblemon properties), within `gate.roamRadius` blocks. Uncatchable, invulnerable, battles against them are cancelled. They despawn when nobody is around and come back when a player is within 48 blocks.
- **Frequency**: `data/cobblemon_safari/worldgen/structure_set/safari_gate.json` (spacing 48 chunks, separation 20). Override it in a datapack to change it, or point the biome tag `has_structure/safari_gate` to nothing to disable the structure. Only new chunks are affected.
- **Operators** can build their own gate with the creative Safari Gate block (unbreakable in survival): the roaming Pokémon settle at any gate up to 8 blocks wide; no warden comes with it. To give one a warden anyway: `/data merge block <x> <y> <z> {keeps_warden:1b}` on any of its pane blocks.
- The template is built by hand in game and saved with a structure block, its bottom layer being the ground (nothing under it; a pond is at most one block deep) and with the air above the clearing height turned to structure void; its pane blocks carry `keeps_warden:1b` and `clear_trees:1b` (added with a typed NBT edit: a gate re-saved in game has lost `clear_trees` once it ticked, put it back in the saved file with an NBT editor, on every `cobblemon_safari:safari_gate` block entity; setting it in game with `/data` would make the gate clear its surroundings within seconds, before the save). `tools/safari_gate_assets.py` only writes the warden's skin now (`--structure` regenerates the old generated lodge, overwriting the hand-built one).
- **Logging in** inside the Safari without a session sends the player out, creative and spectator included (the `exemptCreativeAndSpectator` exemption only applies to players already connected). A player who gets in another way without a session (a waystone, another mod's teleport, a command) is sent back within a second to where they last stood outside, whatever their banked time: a hunt only starts at a gate or with `/safari start`.
- **Spawning gamerule**: with Cobblemon's `doPokemonSpawning` set to false, the Safari spawns no Pokémon at all, specials included.

## Safari points and the shop

Every capture inside the Safari earns Safari points (`points` section: by the species' rarity in the Safari, plus a bonus for today's specials and for shinies: 10, 20, 50 or 120 points, +50 for a special, +250 for a shiny), a currency separate from Safari time. With the bounties (30 per Pokémon of a type mission, 120 for a species, 250 for a special, 200 once all are done) the defaults give about 600 points per hour of active hunting, enough for a first-stage Pokémon of the shop in about a quarter of an hour. Files written before version 18 had about five times less; values still on those old defaults are raised on the next start, values changed by hand are kept. Points always show with their logo, a gold coin glyph of the mod's font (`assets/cobblemon_safari/font/icons.json`, `points/SafariPointsIcon`), in chat, the shop and on the board.

Two shopkeepers open the shop: the Safari Warden at every Safari Gate (he holds a Safari Ball) and the Safari Clerk behind the counter of every rest house (NPC class `safari_clerk`, put back automatically when a loaded house has none, `structure/RestHouseClerk`). The clerk also lists the current missions and, after a confirmation, lets a hunter leave the Safari early (the same as `/safari leave`). `/safari leave` also walks out a player standing in the Safari without a game (a creative or spectator player exempt from the rules), to the world spawn. Their dialogue opens a prize-counter screen over a pixel-art safari landscape that follows the time of day in game: morning, dusk or night (`textures/gui/shop_background_<scene>.png`, drawn by `tools/gui_assets.py`), each with a trainer and original creatures of the mod: tabs (by default Pokémon, Items, Poké Balls), then cards with gently swaying animated Cobblemon models or enlarged items, 8 per page; a name too long for its card scrolls back and forth.

`config/cobblemon_safari/shop.json` (version 10) lists `tabs` (`id`, optional `name` shown as is, `icon` item id; built-in ids `pokemon`, `items`, `balls`, `tickets` have translated names) and `entries`: items (`"type": "item"`, `item`, `count`) and Pokémon (`"type": "pokemon"`, Cobblemon properties in `pokemon`), each with a `price` in points and a `tab`; fields of the other type are left out of the file. A Pokémon entry may set `"price": "auto"` instead of a number: it is then priced like the random Pokémon (its rarity in the Safari times its evolution stage, from the `rotating` base prices), recomputed whenever the shop is listed, so it follows those prices (`config/ShopPriceAdapter`, resolved in `SafariShop.catalogue`). An item entry with `"auto"` is removed (nothing to price it from), and any other text makes the file unreadable until fixed (the log names the value). An entry without a valid tab goes to the one matching its type (ids ending in `_ball` go to `balls`); a tab without entries is hidden. Select a card, then press Buy; the server re-checks everything (points, being at a gate or in a rest house, unchanged price); a Pokémon goes to the party, or the PC when it is full. `/safari shop` opens it at a gate or in a rest house (anywhere for operators), `/safari points` shows the balance, `/safari points add <players> <amount>` (op) gives points. `/safari reload` re-reads the shop file.

By default the fixed entries sell a single Pokémon, a level 45 Tauros for 200 points, plus items and Poké Balls (item cards show the stack size in the corner), and in the Cooking tab what the rest houses' Poké Pot needs: Cobblemon's Hearty Grains, Vivichoke, Medicinal Leek, Tasty Tail, Galarica Nuts, Pep-Up Flowers and Energy Roots, with honey, sugar and milk (added once to older shop files, version 11). When the tabs do not fit their row, they first tighten (smaller icon and padding, the row using the whole box width), then drop their icons and keep their names, and if even that is too wide the row scrolls with arrows at both ends; no tab is ever hidden. After the fixed entries, the shop sells `rotating.count` random Pokémon (15 by default) that change with each rotation of the specials, the same for every player (`shop/RotatingShop`, deterministic like the specials). They are drawn among the species that spawn naturally in the Safari, with the rarities listed in `rotating.rarities` (common, uncommon and rare by default; base prices 250, 500, 500 and 2500 by default, the rare one lowered from 1000 in version 9 so that rarity does not outweigh strength), sold at the lowest level they spawn at in the Safari (so an evolved Pokémon is never sold below its evolution level), in `rotating.tab`. Their price is the base price of their rarity (`commonPrice`, `uncommonPrice`, `rarePrice`, `ultraRarePrice`) times a multiplier for their evolution stage (`RotatingShop.STAGE_MULTIPLIERS`: ×1 first stage, ×2 second, ×3 third; a species alone in its line counts as a second stage), rounded to 50: a spawn bucket says how often a Pokémon appears, not how strong it is, and Cobblemon files many final evolutions as common. A Pokémon the fixed entries already sell is never drawn: same species and same form (compared by Cobblemon's Showdown id, e.g. `vulpixalola`), whatever its level, so no Pokémon is listed twice, while another form of the species (a plain Vulpix next to a fixed Alolan one) still can be. `excludeSpecials` leaves out the current specials, `allowSpecialSpecies` allows legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox species, `excludedSpecies` lists species never drawn, `rotating.enabled` turns them off. The items and balls tabs work the same way (`rotatingItems`, `rotatingBalls`, `shop/RotatingItems`): each rotation draws `count` items (8 and 6 by default) among those its `prices` rules cover, plus its `always` items (Safari Balls by default), leaving out `excluded` ids and `#tags` and anything the fixed entries already sell. Each rule names an item or an item `#tag`, the stack sold and its price; the first rule matching an item wins, so specific items go before their tags. The defaults price Cobblemon's own tags (medicine, candies, mints, mochis, battle items, gems, evolution stones and items, held items; balls by tier) against about 600 points per hour of hunting, and ban the Ability Patch, Sinistcha's teapots and teacups, and the vanilla bone and snowball that Cobblemon's held-item tag contains. A purchase made with a screen opened before a new draw is refused ("the shop changed"), so nobody buys a Pokémon they never saw.

## Bounties (missions of the rotation)

With each rotation of the specials, `bounties.count` missions are drawn (deterministically, from the rotation and the world's salt, so they survive restarts): catch one of the current specials, catch N Pokémon of a type, catch a given species (two of a common one, one of an uncommon one). They are the same for every player and drawn only from species that spawn in the Safari as common or uncommon, so each can be done within one rotation. Each pays its reward in Safari points once per player, plus `bounties.allDoneBonus` when all are done. `/safari bounties` lists them in chat, with the player's progress (the wall screens' BOUNTIES page shows them too). Progress is saved per player and per rotation (`data/cobblemon_safari_bounties.dat`): unfinished missions are lost when the rotation changes.

## Escape Rope

Lent on entry (`escapeRope.enabled`) and taken back on exit, never worn out. Used in a cave of the Safari (no sky above), it brings the player to the surface right above; it does nothing in the open air, during a battle or outside the Safari. Any rope found outside the Safari is removed.

## Daily map reset

At the first server start of each new day (`reset.hour`, `reset.timeZone`), the Safari dimension folder is deleted before the levels load and regenerated with a new terrain seed (a salt mixed into the world seed for that dimension only, `ChunkMapMixin`). Rest houses are rebuilt on the new map; players who logged out inside are moved to the arrival point. Sessions, statistics and banked time are kept. State: `<world>/cobblemon_safari_map.json`. `/safari reset` (op) forces a reset at the next start. Turn it off with `reset.enabled`.
